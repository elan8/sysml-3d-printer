The system of interest is a **desktop fused-filament 3D printer** for an engineering team that prints functional prototypes: brackets, jigs, housings and fit checks, in PLA, PETG and ABS. Speed, dimensional accuracy and clean surfaces matter, and the printer has to be safe to leave running in a shared office.

The scope is one printer concept and one process (fused filament, a 0.4 mm nozzle). Slicers, filament, the workspace and the people who use the printer are outside the system; they appear here through what the team needs and through the conditions the printer operates in.

This is a showcase model, not a certified design. Its figures are working assumptions, listed with their owners in this document.
