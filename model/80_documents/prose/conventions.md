This document is generated from the SysML v2 model of the printer in this repository. Tables, values and diagrams are read from the model, so they cannot drift from it; the prose around them is written by hand.

**Identifiers.** A customer requirement has an identifier `USR-nnn` and a system requirement `SYS-nnn`. Identifiers are stable: other documents, the trade study and the verification cases refer to them. An assumption is `A-<topic>-n`.

**Requirement wording.** A system requirement states a measurable property of the printer and its limit. A limit is a number in the model's `Limits` package, and the requirement and the analysis use the same value. Where a requirement says "estimate", the figure the design is judged by is a first estimate from a simple model and a simulation is named to replace it.

**Units.** SI units and their usual multiples. Temperatures are written in degrees Celsius in text; the model writes them in kelvin because the SysML standard library defines the degree Celsius only as a temperature difference.

**Status of the figures.** Every number that is not a requirement limit is a working assumption (see the assumptions in the Customer Requirements Specification), to be replaced by a sourced or measured value. Nothing in this document claims compliance with a regulation or a standard.
