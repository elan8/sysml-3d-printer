The printer stands on a desk or bench in an office or laboratory, at 15 to 30 degC, on mains power and without dedicated ventilation. Four parties have an interest in it:

| Party | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Engineer | Designs the part, starts the print and uses the result. Not a printer specialist. |
| Team lead | Pays for the printer and for the time it is busy; wants parts the same day. |
| Technician | Calibrates, services and repairs the printer. |
| Office neighbour | Shares the room: hears it, smells it, and is at risk if it fails. |

The reference print is a 100 mm by 100 mm by 20 mm functional bracket with 0.2 mm layers and 0.42 mm lines at up to 300 mm/s, in PETG at 240 degC with the bed at 80 degC. It uses about 12 cm^3 of material. The figures of the system requirements are sized against it.
