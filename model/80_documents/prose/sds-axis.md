One motion axis is modelled in detail: a drive, a 20-tooth belt pulley of 6.3662 mm radius, the belt as a spring and the carriage as a mass, with the figures of the lead candidate: a 0.55 kg carriage with a 0.12 kg hotend on a belt path of 1.26e5 N/m. A CoreXY pair of motors is modelled as one motor on the equivalent axis.

The stepper motor is a class of the Elan8 domain library: a two-phase hybrid stepper on two current-regulated bridges, with a commanded field angle and a speed-dependent torque. Its Modelica behaviour is checked against references derived without a simulator (a held pulley against the square root of k over m, a Runge-Kutta integration of the whole axis, and the eigenfrequencies of the two-mass two-spring system); the model composed in SysML and run with Run42 gives the same trajectory as the Modelica test to 4e-13 m.

**The stepper is part of the axis.** A stepper that holds its position is also a spring, and its rotor is a mass. At the belt, its holding stiffness is 5.5e5 N/m, 4.4 times the belt's, and the rotor's inertia reflected to the belt is 0.14 kg, a fifth of the carriage. Together they make the axis a two-mass, two-spring system whose first resonance is **62.08 Hz, 10 percent below the 69 Hz** the trade study estimates from the belt and the moving mass alone, and whose second is 352 Hz. Both clear the 50 Hz of SYS-005, but the estimate was optimistic, and the margin a shaper works with is smaller. A drive that holds its position through a position loop instead of the field angle does not have this term and resonates at 61.5 Hz (servo) and 63.5 Hz (closed-loop stepper) for the gains in the firmware.

The same axis is built with a brushless servo as `ServoAxis`, using the dq-frame permanent-magnet motor of the domain library.
